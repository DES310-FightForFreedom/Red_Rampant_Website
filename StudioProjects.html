    Red Rampant Studios was never meant to be just about one game following a boy from the Scottish Highlands during the wars of Independence. It was also born to be a creative haven for brilliant story telling and artistic innovation.

    Here are some of the other projects we've spun up over the years.

    What if investigating Scottish myths and folklore was a point-and-click adventure game? Dual-perspective gameplay follows a war journalist and his free-spirited mother as they take notes and pictures of the Scottish wilds and the even wilder beasts hidden beneath... or is it all just tourist-baiting fairy tales?

    When you play Wacko Stack, you decide how the story's spun.

    If you like relaxing cognitive journeys through breathtaking, human-made art-scapes, pop the kettle on and give Wacko Stack a try.

    Clockpocalypse

    A frantic, time-bending survival party game where every second counts. Complete wacky, world-ending challenges against the clock to save humanity from the Clockpocalypse.

    This standalone project let the team channel our chaotic side into an ice-breaker team-building party game that we aim to publish as a mobile app.

    Hook Line and Cyber

    An interactive e-learning course on phishing and cyber security. Play the role of an investigator, with a plucky noir head of IT in your ear every step of the way.

    This project was commissioned by Cyphr, a leading e-learning company that believed in Red Rampant Studios' vision to adapt to the Attention Economy through gamification and interactive education.

    Here and There

    Over the years, Red Rampant Studios Co-Founder Jake McCullagh has created an album of songs, scribbled away in spare time for the love of the game.

    For now, the songs are unlisted on YouTube, but the goal is to get the album up on Spotify.

    The name Here and There refers to the dotted journey of the songs' creation and the duality of life between living in Scotland and Italy.

    Jake's artist name: Jake Make Colour

    No AI was used to produce any images, text, or music.
